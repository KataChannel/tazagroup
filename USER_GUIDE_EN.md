# 📚 AccessTrade Affiliate Platform - Complete User Guide

## 🎯 Platform Overview
AccessTrade is a comprehensive affiliate marketing platform that helps you earn money by promoting products and services. This guide will help you maximize all platform features and capabilities.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### 1. Account Registration
1. Visit the AccessTrade homepage
2. Click **"Sign Up"** in the top right corner
3. Fill in your information:
   - Valid email address
   - Strong password (minimum 8 characters, uppercase, numbers, special characters)
   - Confirm password
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. Check your email for account verification

### 2. Email Verification
1. Open the email from AccessTrade
2. Click the verification link in the email
3. Your account will be activated and you can log in

### 3. Login
1. Click **"Login"** 
2. Enter your email and password
3. Optionally select **"Remember me"** for convenience

## 🎯 Campaign Management

### Browse Campaigns
1. Go to **"Campaigns"** menu
2. Use filters:
   - **Category**: Fashion, Tech, Food, etc.
   - **Commission Rate**: From low to high
   - **Status**: Open, closing soon
3. View campaign details by clicking on the card

### Apply to Campaigns
1. Click **"Apply"** on the campaign details page
2. Fill out the application form:
   - Website/promotion channel
   - Marketing strategy description
   - Estimated traffic
3. Wait for advertiser approval (usually 1-3 days)

### Manage Favorite Campaigns
- Click ⭐ to add to favorites
- Quick access via **"Favorites"** menu
- Receive notifications for updates

## 🔗 Marketing Tools

### 1. Create Affiliate Links
**Access**: Menu → Tools → Link Generator

**Steps**:
1. Select campaign
2. Enter destination URL (landing page)
3. Add UTM parameters (optional):
   - Source: traffic source (facebook, google, website)
   - Medium: media type (cpc, banner, email)
   - Campaign: strategy name
4. Click **"Generate Link"**
5. Copy and use the link

**Tip**: Use meaningful UTM names for easy performance tracking

### 2. Generate QR Codes
**Use when**: Sharing on print media, offline events

**Steps**:
1. Enter your created affiliate link

4. Download PNG or SVG file

### 3. Deep Link Generator

- Custom landing pages
- Advanced analytics

## 💰 Financial Management

### Balance Tracking
**Dashboard displays**:
- **Available Balance**: Can withdraw immediately
- **Pending**: Not yet due for payment
- **Total Earnings**: All earnings from inception

### Withdrawal Requests
**How to withdraw**:
1. Menu → Payments → Withdraw
2. Enter amount (minimum $50)

- **Default cycle**: Monthly on the 15th
- **Automatic payments**: When reaching minimum threshold
- **View schedule**: Menu → Payments → Payment Schedule

### Transaction History

- Export Excel for tax reporting

## 🎓 Tips & Best Practices

### Optimize Earnings
1. **Choose suitable niche**: Focus on areas you understand
2. **Build trust**: Quality content, honest reviews  
3. **Diversify**: Don't depend on one campaign
4. **A/B test**: Try different approaches

4. **Follow guidelines**: Read each campaign's rules
5. **Quality traffic**: Focus on real, interested users
